# Changelog

## 0.8.0, public alpha (M3)

The first public build. Android only (64-bit ARM, Android 7 or newer).

### Build
- A hangar where you build a ship from hull, armour, shield and hardpoint tiles in several
  shapes and sizes, with snapping, mirror mode, undo, paint, and live stats.
- Thrusters and guns go on hardpoints: gatlings, cannon, flak, rockets (dumb-fire and homing), a
  laser, EMP and mines. Each gun levels up with the kills it takes part in.
- Tap a tile to see what it is, its stats and what it costs.
- Save ships to your library, share them as a file or a share code, and load other players'
  ships.

### Fly
- One-stick (auto-fire) or two-stick controls, left- or right-handed, with pinch zoom.
- Ships lose tiles as they're hit, break apart and explode; cut-off pieces drift away.
- Tap an enemy to make it your guns' target.
- A small roster of enemy ships with their own pilots, from level 1 to 100, and bosses.

### Modes
- **WaveTime**: launch from the bay and survive escalating waves. Loot, wave-clear bonuses and
  power-ups (×2 to ×5 firepower); when your ship goes, the escape pod brings you and your cargo
  home. Spend what you earn in the hangar and the market, buy other ships, and climb the
  scoreboard.
- **Creative**: build anything for free, test it against fights from level 1 to 100, and
  publish ships for the WaveTime shop.
- Several pilots on one phone, each with their own stock and records.
- Story mode is not in this build.

### Sound
- Sound effects for every shot, hit, explosion, pickup and button, an engine that follows the
  throttle, and music for the menu, the hangar and combat.
- Settings → Sound test plays every sound.

### Known gaps
- Story mode, stations and jobs are still to come.
- The art, sound and balance are a first pass, and the enemy roster is small.
